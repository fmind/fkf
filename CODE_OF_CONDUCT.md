# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make this community welcoming, safe, and equitable for everyone. We respect the dignity, rights, and contributions of all people, and extend the same opportunity to participate to everyone who acts in good faith and follows this Code of Conduct.

## Encouraged behaviors

Words and actions can be understood differently across cultures, backgrounds, and native languages. With that in mind, contributors and maintainers agree to:

- Respect the purpose of the project and its community spaces.
- Engage kindly and honestly.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for actions and contributions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Help repair harm when it occurs.
- Act in ways that sustain the community's well-being.

## Restricted behaviors

The following conduct, including threats or promotion of it, is not acceptable:

- **Harassment:** violating stated boundaries or continuing unwanted personal attention after a clear request to stop.
- **Character attacks:** insulting, demeaning, or pejorative remarks directed at a person or group.
- **Stereotyping or discrimination:** judging personality or behavior from identity or immutable traits.
- **Sexualization:** conduct that is inappropriately intimate for the project's professional context.
- **Violating confidentiality:** sharing or acting on private information without permission.
- **Endangerment:** causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
- **Misleading identity:** impersonating another person or evading an enforcement action through a false identity.
- **Failing to credit sources:** presenting another person's contribution or content without appropriate credit.
- **Irresponsible promotion or communication:** posting commercial material outside community norms, or presenting restricted material without appropriate care.
- Any other conduct that threatens the community's well-being.

## Reporting an issue

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation. If an incident may be one, use this repository's [private advisory form](https://github.com/fmind/fkf/security/advisories/new); conduct reports are explicitly accepted there even when no software vulnerability is involved. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Relevant messages, logs, or witness accounts may be requested. Safety and confidentiality take priority; investigation and enforcement happen privately unless the affected people agree that a broader communication is useful. The maintainer must recuse themself from a report involving their own conduct when another suitable reviewer is available.

## Addressing and repairing harm

The response depends on impact, context, and prior behavior. Lower steps may be skipped for a serious incident.

1. **Warning:** a private written warning, with clarification of expectations and an appropriate repair such as an apology or acknowledgement of responsibility.
1. **Temporarily limited activities:** a time-limited restriction on particular channels or interactions, with clear conditions for return.
1. **Temporary suspension:** removal from project spaces for a defined period after repeated or serious misconduct, with explicit return conditions.
1. **Permanent ban:** removal from all project spaces when earlier measures did not stop repeated harm, or when no lesser action can keep the community safe.

This ladder is guidance, not a promise that every case starts at the first step. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and centered on safety and repair.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public or private spaces.
